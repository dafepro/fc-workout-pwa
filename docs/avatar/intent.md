# Zoomigo Avatar System — Intent

## Purpose

Zoomigo avatars should become a major part of how players express themselves, recognize teammates, and enjoy coming back to the app.

The new system should move beyond the current simple avatar model and support animated 3D characters with a large set of mix-and-match customization options. Most customization should be earned through participation, consistency, team activity, and special events rather than used as a measure of player skill or rank.

The avatar should feel like **the player's character inside Zoomigo**, not just a profile picture.

---

## Core Intent

The avatar system should:

1. **Give every player a strong sense of ownership.**  
   Players should be able to build a character that feels distinct and personal.

2. **Create a deep pool of meaningful unlockable rewards.**  
   Avatar items should provide a long-term reward system that can keep expanding without changing the core training experience.

3. **Make Zoomigo feel more alive.**  
   Characters should move, react, celebrate, idle, and interact with the world rather than appear as static icons.

4. **Strengthen the social feel of the team.**  
   Players should quickly recognize teammates by their characters in lounges, team spaces, celebrations, and other shared views.

5. **Reward participation without turning appearance into a leaderboard.**  
   Customization should reflect time, consistency, events, interests, and choices. It should not imply that one child is a better athlete because their avatar looks more impressive.

6. **Support years of expansion.**  
   The system should allow new clothing, accessories, animations, themes, props, effects, and seasonal content to be added without rebuilding the avatar model.

---

## Desired Player Experience

A player should be able to open Zoomigo and immediately recognize their avatar as **their character**.

Over time, the character should change with them.

A player may unlock a new pair of shoes after a training streak, earn a funny hat during a team event, choose a new celebration after completing a challenge, or find a rare cosmetic in a prize box. These rewards should give players small reasons to return and should make completed activities feel connected to something visible.

Customization should feel playful rather than technical. A player should be able to make changes quickly, see the result at once, and return to what they were doing.

The ideal reaction is:

> “I want to see what I unlocked and put it on my character.”

Not:

> “I need to manage an inventory.”

---

## Character Style

The avatar should use a stylized 3D look rather than realism.

Characters should feel:

- friendly
- athletic
- expressive
- colorful
- readable on a phone screen
- recognizable from a distance
- appropriate for kids roughly ages 9–12
- consistent with Zoomigo's playful soccer identity

The design should avoid realistic body simulation or appearance systems that encourage players to compare height, weight, body shape, attractiveness, or physical development.

The character model should have enough personality to feel alive while remaining simple enough that clothing and accessories remain easy to read.

---

## Customization Goals

The system should support a large number of combinations from a manageable library of assets.

Useful customization categories may include:

- hair and head styles
- skin appearance
- face options
- shirts and jerseys
- shorts and pants
- socks
- shoes and cleats
- hats and headwear
- glasses and face accessories
- wrist and arm accessories
- backpacks or small carried items
- soccer-themed gear
- mascot-themed items
- costume pieces
- badges or patches
- colors and patterns
- idle poses
- emotes
- celebrations
- entrance animations
- victory animations
- movement styles
- small visual effects
- companion or mascot items, if added later

Not every category needs to exist at launch.

The system should favor **combinable parts** over complete fixed outfits so that a modest number of assets can create many distinct characters.

---

## Unlock Philosophy

Avatar customization is one of Zoomigo's main reward surfaces.

Unlocks should come from several sources so that no single behavior controls the entire system.

Possible sources include:

- completing planned workouts
- maintaining consistency
- personal momentum
- team momentum
- weekly participation
- team challenges
- practice attendance
- prize boxes
- seasonal events
- coach-created events
- team milestones
- special achievements
- limited-time community events
- surprise rewards

The system should reward **showing up and taking part** more often than raw athletic performance.

A player who is slower, newer, injured, or less skilled should still have a rich path to customization.

Rare items can exist, but scarcity should create delight rather than social status pressure.

---

## Unlock Types

Rewards should not be limited to clothing.

A broad reward pool gives Zoomigo more ways to surprise players without flooding the wardrobe with nearly identical items.

Unlockable content may include:

### Appearance

Clothing, shoes, hats, accessories, patterns, colors, and costume pieces.

### Animation

Celebrations, idle animations, emotes, poses, dances, entrances, reactions, and movement styles.

### Effects

Small trails, sparkles, soccer-ball effects, team-colored accents, or other restrained visual flourishes.

### Props

Items the avatar can hold, kick, bounce, wear, or use during certain animations.

### Social Expression

Quick-chat reactions, gestures, team celebrations, or paired animations that remain safe and lightweight.

### Lounge Interaction

Cosmetics or animations that affect how the player's avatar appears or behaves inside the multiplayer team lounge.

---

## Animation and Personality

Animation should be a core part of the new system.

At minimum, avatars should support:

- idle movement
- walking or running
- turning
- simple reactions
- celebrations
- emotes

Over time, the system should support richer animation sets such as juggling a ball, stretching, bouncing on the toes, checking a soccer ball, celebrating with teammates, or reacting to lounge objects.

Animation should add personality without making the app noisy.

Players should not need to trigger every animation manually. Some subtle idle behavior can make characters feel alive while they wait in a lounge or appear on a team screen.

---

## Use Across Zoomigo

The avatar should be a shared identity system used throughout the product.

The same character should appear consistently in places such as:

- player profile
- workout completion
- streak or momentum views
- prize boxes
- team lounge
- team celebrations
- challenge results
- coach-facing player views where useful
- future multiplayer activities
- future social or shared team spaces

Unlocking or changing an item should update the player's appearance everywhere.

The avatar should become one of the visual links that makes separate Zoomigo features feel like one product.

---

## Multiplayer Presence

The avatar system should work especially well inside the Zoomigo team lounge.

Players should be able to recognize teammates without needing large name labels.

Characters should remain readable when many players share the screen at once.

Customization must not break collision, movement, interaction, or other gameplay behavior. Cosmetic differences should change appearance and personality without changing competitive ability.

No cosmetic item should grant a gameplay advantage.

---

## Safe Identity and Expression

Players should have meaningful choices without exposing them to risky social features.

The system should avoid:

- free-form text on clothing
- custom uploaded images
- unrestricted user-created skins
- sexualized clothing or poses
- realistic body-shape controls
- appearance scoring
- public popularity metrics tied to avatars
- trading systems that can create pressure, scams, or disputes
- cosmetic advantages tied to spending between teammates

Players should be able to create distinct characters while staying inside a curated set of Zoomigo-safe assets.

---

## Relationship to Progression

Avatar rewards should support Zoomigo's larger progression system rather than become a separate game layered on top of it.

The avatar answers a simple question:

**“What did I get for showing up?”**

Progress should create visible changes to the player's character, but players should keep control over what they wear.

A newly unlocked item should feel like an option, not a forced upgrade.

There should be no single “best” outfit.

Old items should stay useful because style is based on preference rather than power.

---

## Content Depth

The long-term value of the system comes from combinations.

Zoomigo should be able to create many rewards by mixing:

- reusable base models
- color variants
- patterns
- accessory slots
- themed collections
- animation sets
- seasonal content
- event-specific content

The system should make it cheap to add one more interesting reward.

A new asset should ideally work with most existing characters and outfits instead of requiring a custom character model.

---

## Collections and Themes

Items can be grouped into loose collections to make unlocks more memorable.

Examples:

- Street Soccer
- Space Striker
- Goalkeeper
- Zoomi
- Retro Football
- Snow Day
- Summer Training
- Halloween
- Team Colors
- Arcade
- Explorer
- Superfan
- Trick Shot

Collections should provide a theme, not require players to complete a set.

Mixing pieces from different collections should be encouraged.

---

## Team Identity

Individual expression should sit alongside team identity.

Teams may have shared items such as:

- team jerseys
- team colors
- special warmups
- event shirts
- badges
- tournament items
- team celebration animations

These can create a sense of belonging without making every player look identical.

A player should still look like themselves while wearing team gear.

---

## Performance Intent

The richer avatar system must not make Zoomigo feel slow.

The visual target is “3D and alive,” not maximum rendering detail.

The system should be designed so that:

- avatars load quickly on common phones
- several avatars can appear together
- animations remain smooth
- cosmetics do not require large downloads
- lower-powered devices can reduce visual detail
- the multiplayer lounge can show a full team without becoming unstable

The best avatar system is one players see often. Visual quality is not useful if the product avoids showing avatars because they are too expensive to render.

---

## Accessibility

Customization should not depend only on color.

Items, unlock states, and selections should remain clear for players with color-vision differences.

Menus should use large touch targets and simple categories.

Animations should respect reduced-motion preferences where possible.

The system should also avoid flashing or strong repeated effects.

---

## Non-Goals

The avatar system is not intended to become:

- a realistic human character creator
- a fashion marketplace
- a competitive equipment system
- an NFT or tradable-item economy
- a full RPG inventory system
- a source of stat bonuses
- a popularity contest
- a replacement for the training experience
- a complex 3D game editor

Zoomigo should have deep personalization without requiring players to spend large amounts of time managing it.

---

## MVP Intent

The first version should prove four things:

1. A player can create a recognizable 3D character.
2. The character can wear several independent cosmetic items.
3. The character can play a small set of animations.
4. Zoomigo can grant a new cosmetic or animation as an unlock and immediately make it available to the player.

The MVP does not need a huge asset library.

It needs a strong foundation that makes adding the next 100 items straightforward.

---

## Success Criteria

The system is working when:

- players can recognize their own avatar at a glance
- teammates look meaningfully different from one another
- players use newly unlocked items
- avatar rewards make workout completion feel more rewarding
- players return to change or inspect their character
- the same avatar works across profile, rewards, and multiplayer spaces
- new cosmetics can be added without new application logic
- the system performs well with a full team on common mobile devices
- customization remains fun without creating pressure around athletic skill, appearance, or spending

---

## Guiding Principle

**Make the avatar a living record of participation, personality, and team belonging.**

It should be fun to earn things, fun to change things, and fun to see teammates show up looking different every week.
