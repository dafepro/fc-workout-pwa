# Reward hardening manual test

Use disposable development accounts and test rewards only. Do not upload a
photo containing a person or private information.

## Daily Drop inside Prize Boxes

1. Sign in as a player who has not claimed today's box.
2. Open **Me → Prize boxes**.
3. Confirm **Daily freebie** offers **Claim daily box** without asking for a
   workout.
4. Claim it. Confirm the card says it was added and **Your boxes** increases by
   one without revealing an item.
5. Leave the page, return, and confirm the unopened box remains.
6. Open it. Confirm the reveal shows actual item art, name, rarity, and its
   Avatar or Team Lounge destination.
7. Reload and confirm the item appears in **Recently earned** and **All prizes**.

Expected: claiming and opening are separate, a repeated claim does not add a
second daily box, and workout rewards remain independent.

## Earned destinations

1. Open an Avatar reward and follow **Use in Avatar**.
2. Equip and save it, then open Team Lounge and reload.
3. Open a Team Lounge stamp and follow **Use in Team Lounge**.
4. Place it in an available slot, reload, and confirm it remains editable.

Expected: the saved Avatar renders in the lounge and earned stamps remain
available even when Canvas developer controls are enabled.

## Team Reward concern

1. As staff, publish a harmless development reward.
2. As a player, open **Team**, expand **Report a concern**, select one predefined
   reason, and send it.
3. Confirm the selector collapses and the private-review confirmation appears.
4. As a platform operator, open **Reward concerns**. Confirm the reward and
   reason appear without the reporter's identity.
5. Choose **Hide reward**, confirm the action, and verify the report becomes
   resolved.
6. Return to the player's Team view and reload.

Expected: the hidden reward is no longer shown to players, while the reward,
report, resolution, and moderation audit remain available to authorized staff.
