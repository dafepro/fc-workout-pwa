# The staff console answers on the same hostname as the player app. Access used
# to cover all of /staff and kept crawlers out as a side effect; it now covers
# only /staff/admin, so the coach console and the setup page are reachable and
# have to say this for themselves. Sitting a password form on a crawlable path
# is also how a small domain earns a Safe Browsing interstitial.
#
# This is a request, not a control. The pages carry `noindex` as well, and
# neither is what keeps anyone out -- that is staff sign-in and the API's
# per-request authorization.
User-agent: *
Disallow: /staff
Disallow: /login
